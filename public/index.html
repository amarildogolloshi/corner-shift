Corner Shift

Slide red tiles to the red gate and white tiles to the white gate. If the path is clear, a correct tile can slide through its corner and exit in one move.

Moves: 0
Tiles Left: 25

Drag, swipe, or tap a tile. Tiles can slide through empty spaces. A correct tile can exit through its gate if it is already on the gate line and every space between it and the corner is empty.
